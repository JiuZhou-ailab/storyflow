# Storyflow first practice — 180 seconds

Real native UI is the primary visual. A single sample project connects requirements, reading, one edit and a saved version. The opening states the outcome; each chapter shows an action and its success signal. Pre-authored example conversations remain visibly disclosed throughout. No staged generation is presented as live.

Palette: warm near-white #F5F5F3, ink #202124, secondary #53565D, one blue accent #2456D6. System Chinese sans-serif, resolved on the macOS renderer. 2560 × 1440 landscape, preserve native 16:10 screen geometry. Keep captions outside the screen. Use restrained 400–600ms transitions and slow focus zooms; no bouncing, decorative glows or busy background loops. Long holds are intentional reading time with narration, not missing animation.

Chinese synthetic narration with short readable captions; no background music competing with instructions. Intro/outro use large left-aligned type. UI stages use a slim chapter header, large screen, bottom captions, and a small persistent sample disclosure. Real click positions may receive a subtle brief ring in the edit.
